Specialized Nutrition Recommendations

Pregnancy Nutrition

  • Folate-rich foods: Spinach, lentils, fortified cereals
  • Iron sources: Lean red meat, beans, fortified bread
  • Calcium-rich: Dairy products, leafy greens, almonds
  • Omega-3: Salmon, walnuts, chia seeds
  • Protein: Eggs, lean poultry, tofu

Pediatric Nutrition

  • Whole grains: Brown rice, whole wheat bread, oats
  • Fruits & Vegetables: Colorful variety for vitamins
  • Dairy: Milk, yogurt, cheese for calcium
  • Lean proteins: Fish, chicken, beans
  • Healthy snacks: Nuts, fruits, whole grain crackers

Renal/Kidney Health

  • Low sodium: Fresh herbs, garlic, lemon juice
  • Limited potassium: Apples, berries, cabbage
  • Controlled protein: Fish, egg whites, small portions
  • Low phosphorus: Rice, pasta, fresh fruits
  • Fluid management: Monitor intake as advised

Fitness & Wellness

  • Pre-workout: Bananas, oats, whole grain toast
  • Post-workout: Protein smoothies, Greek yogurt
  • Hydration: Water, coconut water, herbal teas
  • Recovery foods: Lean meats, quinoa, sweet potatoes
  • Anti-inflammatory: Berries, fatty fish, turmeric
